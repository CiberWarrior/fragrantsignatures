Natural perfume creators from the ex-Yugoslavia region have founded the association <a href="https://aromaterapija.info/vodjeni-nosom/pomirisi-ovo-udruga-kreatora-prirodnih-parfema/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-accent-600 underline hover:text-accent-800 transition-colors">Fragrant Signatures</a>. Creators from Croatia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Slovenia have joined. We are in the process of developing a work plan and collecting ideas for lectures that we will include in our further education. Our socializing is going great, the atmosphere is optimistic and enthusiastic.

We plan to engage in educating the public about the good practice of paying attention to the activities of our own nose as a method of promoting well-being and improving the quality of life. We want to popularize the idea that the scents we surround ourselves with should be of natural origin.

We intend to find ways to get involved in various festivals, tourist events, libraries' programs, fairs, etc.

And we will certainly try to network with similar initiatives around the world! For now, the most promising collaboration is with the Turkish Aromatherapy Association. If we find good topics and ways for joint activities, international cooperation is open to us. There are interested creators who have completed some of the levels of the Fragrant Signatures education all over the globe!

And we have a lot to learn from societies where the smell culture is far more developed than ours!
